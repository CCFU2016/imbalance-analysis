Key Takeaways

  • A 1 MW asset earned between 581k and 2,058k EUR annually over the past 5 years — averaging 987k EUR/year.
  • The probability of a negative return drops rapidly with time: a 24h loss occurs 1–10% of the time, but a 14-day loss has never occurred in 5 years of data.
  • The worst single loss event was -5,279 EUR in 2024 (29 hours over April 6–7) — less than 1% of that year's annual return.
  • 2024 was the riskiest year by every measure: highest loss probability, deepest drawdown, and longest negative streak (7.9 days). 2022 was the most profitable and least risky.
  • Even the longest negative streaks resulted in negligible cumulative losses (under 52 EUR) — they are long but shallow.
